### 📱 **SCROLL BEHAVIORS**

**Navigation:**
- Scrolling DOWN past 150px → Nav hides
- Scrolling UP → Nav reappears
- Mouse near top edge → Nav appears
- Past 80px scroll → Nav becomes compact

**Elements Appearing:**
- Fade in when 15% of element is visible
- Each type has unique animation:
  - Cards: Fade up + scale
  - Timeline: Slide left
  - Text blocks: Fade up
  - Images: Fade right
